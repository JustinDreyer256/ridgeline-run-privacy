Ridgeline Run Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Ridgeline Run (“the game”) is a mobile game made by Justin Dreyer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and I don’t collect your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional video ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID to show and measure those ads.
  • If you buy something in the game, Google Play handles the payment. I never see your payment details.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: scores, embers and stones, unlocked items, upgrades, missions and your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics). This information stays on your device. It isn’t sent to me, and I can’t see it.

It’s deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone’s settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to revive after a fall or to double the embers from a run. You choose whether to watch them. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which is run by Google.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • information about your device and the game, such as the device model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

Google’s use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The game asks AdMob to show only family-appropriate ads (rated PG or lower).

Your ad choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent message before any ads, so you can choose how your information is used for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On any Android phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-game purchases (Google Play)

The game sells optional packs of spirit stones. Purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. The game receives only a confirmation of which pack was bought so it can add the stones to your save. I never receive your name, email or payment details.

Device permissions

  • Internet: to load ads and process purchases. The game itself doesn’t send your information anywhere.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact me and I’ll help with whatever is needed.

Keeping and deleting information

I don’t keep any information about you. To delete your game progress, uninstall the game or clear its data. To limit what Google uses for ads, use the ad choices above, or see Google’s privacy policy for your rights over information Google holds.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because I don’t collect or store personal information, there is normally nothing for me to provide. For information held by Google for advertising or purchases, use Google’s tools linked above. You can always contact me with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
